# About — Dorothy Dean Designs

*Faith is the foundation. Black culture is the expression. Peace is the outcome.*

---

## Our Story

Dorothy Dean Designs began with a simple conviction: what you wear should remind you who you are becoming.

We are a lifestyle apparel brand rooted in faith, shaped by Black culture, and committed to peace, self-worth, and legacy. Every piece we make is a wearable reminder — of faith, identity, confidence, and purpose. Not loud. Not trend-chasing. Intentional.

We create for the faith-driven who refuse to choose between their belief and their style. For the ones building something that outlasts them.

---

## What We Stand For — LIFT

LIFT is the heartbeat of everything we design.

- **L — Legacy.** Honoring the history, the culture, and the shoulders we stand on.
- **I — Identity.** Self-worth and confidence. Who you are, and who you are becoming.
- **F — Faith.** Spiritual grounding. Quiet strength, not performance.
- **T — Tomorrow.** *(Coming soon)* Future-focused storytelling for the next generation.

---

## Mission & Values

We create wearable reminders of faith, identity, confidence, and purpose — designed to affirm who you are becoming.

If it doesn't support **faith, identity, confidence, or purpose**, it doesn't ship.

**What we are:** Rooted · Elevated · Intentional · Empowering
**What we are not:** Trend-driven · Loud or gimmicky · Mass-produced in spirit · Performative
