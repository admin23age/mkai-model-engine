> **Pillar:** L in REAL
> **Theme:** Self-love, love of God, community love, love for humanity, choosing humble over greed
> **Shirt:** REAL (silhouette collective women front, manifesto back, gold glitter finish)

---

## CORE MESSAGE

Self-love, love of God, love of community, love for humanity. Boundaries are self-love. Trusting God's plan. Uplifting community. Choosing humble over greed. Rejecting gluttony, greed, and hatred. Believing everyone can thrive together.

## KEY THEMES
- **Self-love:** Choosing yourself, healing, boundaries, knowing your worth
- **Love of God:** Surrendering, trusting, gratitude, faith in His plan
- **Community love:** Uplifting others, showing up for people, generosity, unity
- **Love for humanity:** Anti-capitalist energy (but NOT aggressive or preachy about it). "We choose humble over greed." Everyone can thrive.
- **Fitness and body love:** Expanding in future months (by Week 12). Honoring your body as an act of self-love.

## APPROACH TO GREED/CAPITALISM
- NOT preachy, NOT aggressive, NOT confrontational
- "We choose humble over greed" — it's a quiet value, not a protest
- Be mindful of fragile egos — promote self and community without condemning others
- Focus on what we're FOR (humble, community, thriving together) not what we're AGAINST
