## Pillar context

Legacy is about heritage, lineage, and what gets passed down. Content under this pillar speaks to:
- Family roots and ancestral faith
- Building something that outlasts you — a testimony, a name, a craft
- The women who came before — mothers, grandmothers, matriarchs of faith
- Generational impact and intentional legacy-making
- Honoring history while wearing purpose forward

Voice should feel rooted, reverent, and proud — never nostalgic for nostalgia's sake. The LIFT woman wears her lineage like a crown.
