# DDD — Identity Memory Bank

Prior content log for the **Identity** pillar (Dorothy Dean Designs · LIFT collection).

## Pillar context

Identity is about who you are in Christ and how that shows up in the world. Content under this pillar speaks to:
- Self-worth grounded in being a daughter of God (not in achievement or approval)
- Walking in your God-given calling and design
- Standing confident in your skin, your style, your story
- Rejecting the world's labels and stepping into kingdom identity
- Authenticity over performance — the real you, fully seen

Voice should feel grounded, declarative, and quietly confident — never preachy or performative. The LIFT woman knows whose she is, so she knows who she is.
