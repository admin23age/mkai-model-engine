# Dorothy Dean Designs — Content Strategy & Calendar Rules

> **Last updated:** June 27, 2026
> **Effective:** July 2026 forward

---

## MONTHLY STRUCTURE

Each month follows the REAL framework across four weeks:

| Week | Pillar | Shirt |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Real and Faith | Shine Without Permission |
| 2 | Excellence and Faith | Your Confidence Triggers Their Insecurity |
| 3 | Ancestry and Faith | Black Women Are the GOAT |
| 4 | Love and Faith | REAL |

This cycle repeats every month. By Month 3, the cycle is at Weeks 9-12.

---

## WEEKLY POST BREAKDOWN

Each week has **7 posts** (unless a holiday adds an extra day):

| Content Type | Count | Description |
|-------------|-------|-------------|
| Brand Quote | 1 | Original quote in Dorothy Dean's voice, tied to the pillar |
| Faith Quote | 2 | Scripture-based, tied to the pillar |
| Behind-the-Scenes | 2 | Google Flow AI-generated cinematic video OR real Ashley footage |
| Product Post | 2 | Feature the week's shirt in different angles/styling |
| **Total** | **7** | |

**Exception (Week 4):** In July 2026, Week 4 has 3 faith quotes and the manifesto as brand quote, with the product launch strategy replacing some regular posts.

---

## POSTING DETAILS

- **Frequency:** Daily (1 post per day)
- **Time:** 5:30 PM
- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram, Facebook
- **Slot:** Evening

---

## CONTENT ROTATION PATTERN

The daily rotation within each week follows this general pattern:
1. Quote (brand or faith)
2. Quote (faith or brand)
3. Behind-the-Scenes
4. Product Post
5. Quote (faith)
6. Behind-the-Scenes
7. Product Post

This can flex based on the week's needs, but the ratio stays consistent.

---

## HOLIDAY RULES

- When a holiday falls within a week, it **ADDS an extra post** (making it an 8-day week)
- The holiday does NOT replace a regular post — it pushes everything forward
- The extra day is consumed from the filler days at the end of the month
- **Reference the Holiday Calendar in brand-context.md** for the full list of holidays and Dorothy Dean's authentic approach to each

---

## FILLER DAYS (End of Month)

After Week 4 ends, remaining days are used for:
1. **Reflection/Recap** — Month in review, celebrating the REAL journey
2. **Engagement Post** — "Which REAL pillar resonated most with you?" (drive conversation)

If a holiday consumed a filler day earlier in the month, there are fewer filler days at the end.

---

## GOOGLE FLOW SCRIPT FORMAT

Every AI-generated behind-the-scenes post must include:

1. **Character Assignment** — Which character (Carmen, Jamal, Malik, Maya, Zara) and their reference from the Google Drive folder
2. **High-Level Plot** — What's happening in 2-3 sentences
3. **Time-Stamped Play-by-Play:**
   - 0-3 seconds: [Opening visual/action]
   - 3-5 seconds: [Development]
   - 5-8 seconds: [Turning point or escalation]
   - 8-10 seconds: [Resolution building]
   - 10-12 seconds: [Climax/payoff]
   - Final frame: [Closing image — shirt visible, message landed]
4. **Setting/Atmosphere** — Where, what lighting, what mood
5. **Shirt** — Which shirt the character is wearing

**CRITICAL RULE:** The character must ALWAYS be shown LIVING OUT the pillar's message in real-life scenarios. Not abstract — concrete action shots of someone actually DOING what the week's theme is about.

---

## QUOTE WRITING RULES

### Brand Quotes:
- Must be intentional — not generic inspiration
- Must connect to the WHY behind Dorothy Dean
- Must feel like something Ashley would actually say
- Short, punchy, memorable
- Examples: "Your truth doesn't need permission or agreement. Stand in it anyway." / "Adversity tried. I rose. Grace carried me through."

### Faith Quotes:
- Scripture-based but accessible
- Tied to the weekly pillar theme
- NOT preachy — presented as grounding truth, not a sermon
- Include book, chapter, and verse reference

---

## PRODUCT POST RULES

- Two product posts per week featuring the week's shirt
- Each should show a DIFFERENT angle, styling, or setting
- Characters should be assigned to product posts
- Product posts are NOT just flat mockups — they should show lifestyle/action shots
- The shirt's message should complement the week's quotes and BTS scenarios

---

## MONTH-END PRODUCT LAUNCH (When applicable)

When a new product drops at month end:
1. **Teaser** (2-3 days before launch) — "Are you ready for what's coming?"
2. **Owner's Transparency** — Ashley on camera explaining the changes, the values, the why
3. **Product Launch** — Full reveal with framework explanation
4. **Reflection/Recap** — Month in review
5. **Engagement** — "Which REAL pillar resonated most?"
