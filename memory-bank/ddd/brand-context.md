## WHO IS DOROTHY DEAN?

Dorothy Dean is named after Ashley Galloway's grandmother, Dorothy Dean. The connection is deeply spiritual — through this journey, Ashley has been learning who she is, and her grandmother's name carries that weight. Some days Ashley feels aligned and obedient to God, and some days she doesn't. The brand lives in that tension honestly.

---

## THE FULL STORY — WHY DOROTHY DEAN EXISTS

Ashley has a big heart. She tends to put others before herself, forgives too easily, and doesn't guard her heart the way she should. That openness has gotten her into trouble — emotionally, relationally, professionally. When stress hits, her emotions get out of whack. She crashes out, loses it, feels defeated. But the next morning, she gets back up and keeps going. That's who she is.

Ashley had personal challenges she thought she'd dealt with when she moved to Raleigh. Then she got a job at a global tech company and became the first Black female in her role. Her manager triggered every single one of those unresolved issues.

The company expected Ashley to conduct herself like "every other girl with blonde hair and blue eyes" who came before her. She didn't fit that mold. She never was going to. Sometimes she found herself forgetting who she was — dimming her light, code-switching, questioning her confidence just to try and fit into a space that was never built for her. In the Black community, they say: regardless of how well you fit in, you still have to remember that you're still Black. Ashley forgot that for a while.

The manager was a terrible manager. Instead of addressing concerns directly — instead of saying "hey, you need to get this together" — she chose to quietly push Ashley out and do things behind her back. She crossed the line and took Ashley's personal life into her own hands professionally. There were judgment calls made based on behaviors that were not Ashley's best moments, but instead of honest confrontation, the manager chose deception.

One of the things the manager did: she went to a new VP on his first day and told him that Ashley was a sex offender engaged in sexual harassment. This was not true. According to what Ashley has been told, they altered her drug test and fabricated evidence. The VP called the federal government and the FBI, and that's how the legal case began — a case that has now challenged Ashley's employment and livelihood.

Some of the people involved in this now work in government. It sounds crazy, but it's very real. It can happen.

Ashley's degree is in entrepreneurship. From that point forward, she had to be resourceful and resilient and find other sources of income. But focusing on the business has been incredibly difficult because the anger and frustration from the betrayal are ever-present. Some days it's overwhelming.

**Dorothy Dean was born from that pain.** It's not a brand about having it all figured out. It's about channeling adversity into purpose. It's about faith when things are broken. It's about resilience — not never breaking, but always getting back up. It's about remembering who you are when the world wants you smaller.

Ashley is honest about the fact that she doesn't live this perfectly every day. Some days she's close to God, and some days she's not. Some days her hormones are everywhere, her cycle is coming, and she feels overwhelmed and defeated. That vulnerability is what makes Dorothy Dean authentic — it's not toxic positivity. It's real faith, real struggle, real grace.

**The brand isn't "get over it." It's: I'm broken and I'm still standing. I'm angry and I'm still choosing grace. I'm learning who I am — and some days I mess that up.**

## WHY "SHINE WITHOUT PERMISSION"

"Shine Without Permission" came directly from Ashley's experience as the first Black female in her role. They wanted her to conform. She tried to fit in and lost herself in the process. The shirt is about refusing to dim yourself, remembering who you are, and shining because you're different — not despite it. It's tied to Matthew 5:14: "You are the light of the world. A city on a hill cannot be hidden."

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS FOR CONTENT

Every quote, every scenario, every post must connect back to this story. Not explicitly — Jamal should never tell Ashley's personal story in content. But the TONE, the INTENTION, the VULNERABILITY must come from this place. When you write a quote about resilience, it's not generic. It's about a woman who was betrayed, who crashed out, who felt defeated, and who got back up the next morning. When you write about excellence, it's about working twice as hard in a system that wasn't built for you. When you write about ancestry, it's about reclaiming what was taken.

**If a quote feels like it could come from any brand, it's wrong. If it feels like it could only come from Dorothy Dean, it's right.**

## THE REAL FRAMEWORK (Replaces LIFT)

REAL is the foundational framework. Each pillar is paired with Faith — faith is woven through everything, not a separate category.

### R — Real and Faith
- Being honest with yourself, standing in your truth regardless of who agrees
- Resilience: falling and getting back up, even when you're not at your best spiritually
- Remembering who you are when the world wants you smaller
- **Shirt:** Shine Without Permission

### E — Excellence and Faith
- Black excellence — rising above adversity with grace, perseverance, determination
- Working twice as hard, staying graceful, celebrating Black women's brilliance
- Grace under pressure
- **Shirt:** Your Confidence Triggers Their Insecurity (crop top)

### A — Ancestry and Faith
- Honoring generational power, celebrating Black women's erased contributions
- Understanding your lineage as your superpower
- Recognizing (NOT bragging about) what Black women built
- Biblical women of power: Queen of Sheba, Esther, Ruth
- **Shirt:** Black Women Are the GOAT

### L — Love and Faith
- Self-love and boundaries, knowing your worth
- Love of God — trust, surrender, faith in His plan
- Love for humanity — uplifting community, rejecting greed/gluttony, choosing humble
- Fitness and love for your body (expanding in future months)
- **Shirt:** REAL (silhouette collective women front, manifesto back, gold glitter finish)

## TONE OF VOICE

### Dorothy Dean IS:
- Vulnerable and honest — some days are good, some days are hard. That's real.
- Faith-forward but NOT preachy — not "churchy churchy churchy every day"
- Multidimensional — some days faith, some days culture, some days Black power, some days "let's hug a tree"
- Balanced — never one-note. Content rotates through all four REAL pillars equally (25% each).
- Aspirational but imperfect — she crashes out sometimes. She loses it. But she gets back up.
- Communal — not about individual ego. Collective power, mutual uplift.
- Recognizing, not bragging — especially for Ancestry content.

### Dorothy Dean is NOT:
- Sermon-y or preachy
- Toxic positivity ("just be positive!")
- Aggressive or confrontational about politics or economics
- Surface-level or generic inspiration
- One-dimensional
- Performative
- Dimming its light to make others comfortable

## HOLIDAY APPROACH

Dorothy Dean honors holidays **authentically and from truth** — not the sanitized majority narrative.

**Key rules:**
- **Washington's Birthday:** Leadership engagement — "Build your perfect president." Not celebrating the individual.
- **Memorial Day:** Honor the ORIGINAL creators — formerly enslaved people in Charleston, SC (1865).
- **Columbus Day:** NEVER celebrate Columbus. Reframe as Black leadership celebration or engagement post.
- **Thanksgiving:** Always tell the authentic, honest history.
- **Black Friday:** NOT about consumerism. Reframe toward loyalty, consistently building, contributing to the USA.
- **Valentine's Week:** Focus on Love and Faith (L in REAL) — self-love, God's love, community love.
- **July 4th:** Casual engagement — "What are your plans? What's on your grill?" Acknowledging, not celebratory.
- **Easter:** Full faith celebration — resurrection and hope.
