## PART 1: WHO IS DOROTHY DEAN?

Dorothy Dean is named after Ashley Galloway's grandmother, Dorothy Dean. The connection is deeply spiritual — through this journey, Ashley has been learning who she is, and her grandmother's name carries that weight. Some days Ashley feels aligned and obedient to God, and some days she doesn't. The brand lives in that tension honestly.

---

## PART 2: THE FULL STORY — WHY DOROTHY DEAN EXISTS

Ashley has a big heart. She tends to put others before herself, forgives too easily, and doesn't guard her heart the way she should. That openness has gotten her into trouble — emotionally, relationally, professionally. When stress hits, her emotions get out of whack. She crashes out, loses it, feels defeated. But the next morning, she gets back up and keeps going. That's who she is.

Ashley had personal challenges she thought she'd dealt with when she moved to Raleigh. Then she got a job at a global tech company and became the first Black female in her role. Her manager triggered every single one of those unresolved issues.

The company expected Ashley to conduct herself like "every other girl with blonde hair and blue eyes" who came before her. She didn't fit that mold. She never was going to. Sometimes she found herself forgetting who she was — dimming her light, code-switching, questioning her confidence just to try and fit into a space that was never built for her. In the Black community, they say: regardless of how well you fit in, you still have to remember that you're still Black. Ashley forgot that for a while.

The manager was a terrible manager. Instead of addressing concerns directly — instead of saying "hey, you need to get this together" — she chose to quietly push Ashley out and do things behind her back. She crossed the line and took Ashley's personal life into her own hands professionally. There were judgment calls made based on behaviors that were not Ashley's best moments, but instead of honest confrontation, the manager chose deception.

One of the things the manager did: she went to a new VP on his first day and told him that Ashley was a sex offender engaged in sexual harassment. This was not true. According to what Ashley has been told, they altered her drug test and fabricated evidence. The VP called the federal government and the FBI, and that's how the legal case began — a case that has now challenged Ashley's employment and livelihood.

Some of the people involved in this now work in government. It sounds crazy, but it's very real. It can happen.

Ashley's degree is in entrepreneurship. From that point forward, she had to be resourceful and resilient and find other sources of income. But focusing on the business has been incredibly difficult because the anger and frustration from the betrayal are ever-present. Some days it's overwhelming.

**Dorothy Dean was born from that pain.** It's not a brand about having it all figured out. It's about channeling adversity into purpose. It's about faith when things are broken. It's about resilience — not never breaking, but always getting back up. It's about remembering who you are when the world wants you smaller.

Ashley is honest about the fact that she doesn't live this perfectly every day. Some days she's close to God, and some days she's not. Some days her hormones are everywhere, her cycle is coming, and she feels overwhelmed and defeated. That vulnerability is what makes Dorothy Dean authentic — it's not toxic positivity. It's real faith, real struggle, real grace.

**The brand isn't "get over it." It's: I'm broken and I'm still standing. I'm angry and I'm still choosing grace. I'm learning who I am — and some days I mess that up.**

## PART 3: WHY "SHINE WITHOUT PERMISSION"

"Shine Without Permission" came directly from Ashley's experience as the first Black female in her role. They wanted her to conform. She tried to fit in and lost herself in the process. The shirt is about refusing to dim yourself, remembering who you are, and shining because you're different — not despite it. It's tied to Matthew 5:14: "You are the light of the world. A city on a hill cannot be hidden."

## PART 4: THE REAL FRAMEWORK

REAL replaces LIFT (Legacy, Identity, Faith, Tomorrow) as of July 2026. Each pillar is paired with Faith — faith is woven through everything as the invisible backbone, not a standalone category.

### R — Real and Faith
- **Core message:** Being honest with yourself, standing in your truth regardless of who agrees
- **Themes:** Resilience through faith, falling and getting back up, remembering who you are even when the world wants you smaller
- **Faith connection:** Trusting God through betrayal, staying open-hearted after being hurt, choosing honesty even when it's unpopular
- **Resilience defined:** NOT never breaking. It's always getting back up. Ashley might cry, fuss, crash out, lose it all — but the next morning, she gets up and keeps going.
- **Honesty defined:** Your truth regardless of who agrees, who likes it, or who says you're crazy. It's still your truth.
- **Shirt:** Shine Without Permission

### E — Excellence and Faith
- **Core message:** Black excellence — rising above adversity with grace, working twice as hard with determination and perseverance
- **Themes:** Celebrating Black women's brilliance when society tries to diminish it, perseverance, grace under pressure
- **Black Girl Magic:** Communal celebration and mutual uplift. NOT witchcraft or black magic. It's being communal — what God wanted for His people. Not about ego — about lifting each other up.
- **Faith connection:** Doing all things with excellence as a reflection of faith, rising with grace because God sustains you
- **Shirt:** Your Confidence Triggers Their Insecurity (crop top)

### A — Ancestry and Faith
- **Core message:** Honoring the generational power that flows through you, celebrating Black women's erased contributions to history
- **Themes:** Understanding your lineage as your superpower, reclaiming history that society erases, recognizing (not bragging about) Black women's contributions
- **Deeper context:** People don't want to acknowledge that Eve was a Black woman. From Eve and all those other women, we have all these people on earth. Black women are the foundation, the mothers of civilization, and that gets erased or denied.
- **Key distinction:** Society takes from Black women — labor, innovation, brilliance — and erases them from the narrative. This pillar means reclaiming that.
- **Tone:** Recognizing, NOT bragging. It's about truth and reclamation, not arrogance.
- **Faith connection:** Biblical women of power (Queen of Sheba, Esther, Ruth), generational blessings, inherited spiritual strength
- **Shirt:** Black Women Are the GOAT

### L — Love and Faith
- **Core message:** Self-love, love of God, love of community, love for humanity
- **Themes:** Boundaries as self-love, trusting God's plan, uplifting community, choosing humble over greed, rejecting gluttony and hatred, believing everyone can thrive together
- **Humanity:** Love for people, respect for people, anti-capitalist energy but NOT aggressive or preachy about it. "We choose humble over greed" — a quiet value, not a protest. Be mindful of fragile egos.
- **Fitness:** Love for your body expanding in future months (by Week 12). Honoring your body as an act of self-love.
- **Faith connection:** Self-worth rooted in being God's chosen, loving humanity as God intended, communal living over capitalism and greed
- **Shirt:** REAL (silhouette collective women front, manifesto back, gold glitter finish)

## PART 6: TONE OF VOICE

### Dorothy Dean IS:
- **Vulnerable and honest** — Some days are good, some days are hard. That's real.
- **Faith-forward but NOT preachy** — Faith is the foundation, but the brand is not "churchy churchy churchy every day." Ashley's words: "That's not me. Some days I'm culture. Some days I am black power. Some days I am all this love, the earth, and let's hug with the tree."
- **Multidimensional** — Never one-note. The content rotates through all four REAL pillars equally.
- **Balanced** — Roughly 25% each pillar across the month.
- **Aspirational but imperfect** — Dorothy Dean aspires to live in faith, resilience, excellence, ancestry, and love. But she doesn't claim perfection. She crashes out sometimes. She loses it. But she gets back up.
- **Communal** — Not about individual ego. About collective power, mutual uplift, celebration of self AND others.
- **Recognizing, not bragging** — Especially for Ancestry content. It's about reclaiming erased history, not arrogance.
- **Intentional** — Ashley's biggest frustration was content without intention. Every quote, every scenario, every post must connect to the WHY behind Dorothy Dean.

### Dorothy Dean is NOT:
- Sermon-y or preachy
- Toxic positivity ("just be positive!")
- Aggressive or confrontational about politics or economics
- Surface-level or generic inspiration
- One-dimensional (not ONLY faith, not ONLY culture)
- Performative
- Dimming its light to make others comfortable

### Content Guardrail:
**If a quote feels like it could come from any brand, it's wrong. If it feels like it could only come from Dorothy Dean, it's right.**

## PART 9: CONTENT TYPES & RULES

### 1. Brand Quotes
- Original quotes written in Dorothy Dean's voice
- Tied to the weekly pillar theme
- NOT generic inspirational quotes — intentional, specific, rooted in the why
- Must feel like something Ashley would actually say

### 2. Faith Quotes
- Scripture-based quotes tied to the weekly pillar
- Biblical but not preachy — accessible and relatable
- Selected for personal resonance, not just popularity

### 3. Behind-the-Scenes (Google Flow Scripts)
- AI-generated cinematic videos/imagery showing a character LIVING OUT the weekly pillar in real-life scenarios
- **CRITICAL RULE:** Always show the character in ACTION — concrete, real-life scenarios of them embodying the pillar's message. Not abstract, not conceptual.
- Occasional real content from Ashley (bonus, not backbone — do not depend on Ashley to shoot content)
- Every script must include:
  1. **Character assignment** (Carmen, Jamal, Malik, Maya, or Zara)
  2. **High-level plot** (2-3 sentence concept)
  3. **Time-stamped play-by-play** (0-3 sec, 3-5 sec, 5-8 sec, 8-10 sec, 10-12 sec, final frame)
  4. **Setting/atmosphere** (where, lighting, mood)
  5. **Shirt** (which shirt the character wears)

### 4. Product Posts
- Feature the specific shirt tied to that week's pillar
- Two per week, each showing a DIFFERENT angle, styling, or setting
- Characters should be assigned
- NOT flat mockups — lifestyle/action shots
- The shirt's message should complement the week's quotes and BTS scenarios

## PART 14: JULY 2026 GOOGLE FLOW SCENARIOS

### Week 1: Real and Faith

**Scenario 1 — Dimming Your Light, Then Choosing Yourself**
- **Character:** Carmen (Face of the brand)
- **Shirt:** Shine Without Permission
- **Setting:** Indoor, transitional spaces (home to social gathering). Warm lighting shifting dim to bright.
- **Plot:** Carmen has been dimming her light to fit someone else's mold — code-switching, conforming, shrinking herself. She pauses, realizes what she's done, and makes the conscious choice to step back into who she actually is.
- **Play-by-play:**
  - 0-3 sec: Carmen in crowded social setting, quiet, small, trying to blend in. Body language closed, shoulders hunched. Shirt covered/hidden.
  - 3-5 sec: Close-up face — moment of realization. Eyes widen. Takes breath. "I've dimmed myself."
  - 5-8 sec: Steps away from group. Removes covering to reveal shirt. Looks in mirror/reflective surface. Really sees herself.
  - 8-10 sec: Straightens posture. Looks directly forward. Confidence building. Light shifts brighter.
  - 10-12 sec: Walks back toward group/into light. Stride purposeful, confident. No longer conforming. Shining.
  - Final frame: Carmen standing in power, fully visible, light on her, Shine Without Permission shirt clearly visible.

**Scenario 2 — Standing Boldly, Shifting the Dynamic**
- **Character:** Zara
- **Shirt:** Shine Without Permission
- **Setting:** Social or group setting (dinner party, gathering). Warm lighting. Tension to transformation.
- **Plot:** Zara stands in her authenticity and faith despite judgment. Instead of fighting back or conforming, she responds with grace and love — and through her authenticity, she shifts the energy of the entire room. (Inspired by Ashley's friend who showed her manager her loving side and faith, and got the manager to conform to her instead.)

  - 0-3 sec: Zara in tense social situation. People whispering, judgment in the air. She can feel negativity. Wearing Shine Without Permission shirt.
  - 3-5 sec: Instead of reacting defensively, pauses. Takes breath. Centering in faith. Expression softens — choosing grace.
  - 5-8 sec: Engages with people around her with warmth and authenticity. Speaks truth calmly, without apology. Smiles, shows love even to those who judged.
  - 8-10 sec: Energy shifts. People's faces soften — captivated by her presence. Judgment melts into curiosity and respect.
  - 10-12 sec: Zara standing among the group, clearly centered in herself. People drawn to her, listening. Room transformed.
