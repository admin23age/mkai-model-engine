# Dorothy Dean Designs — Official Brand Guide (Master Source of Truth)
*Source: Branding Guide 1.1 — Last modified Jan 20, 2026*

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

**Brand Statement:**
Dorothy Dean Designs is a lifestyle apparel brand rooted in faith, shaped by Black culture, and committed to peace, self-worth, and legacy.

**Faith is the foundation. Black culture is the expression. Peace is the outcome.**

**Brand Anchor Statement:**
We create wearable reminders of faith, identity, confidence, and purpose—designed to affirm who you are becoming.

> Every decision—design, copy, AI characters, product launches—must reflect this anchor.
> If it doesn't support **faith, identity, confidence, or purpose**, it does not ship.

---

## 2. Brand Pillars — LIFT

LIFT is the core organizing system across the brand, website, and social presence.

- **L — Legacy:** Honoring history, culture, and the shoulders we stand on. Truth, heritage, collective memory.
- **I — Identity:** Self-worth, confidence, personal affirmation. Who you are and who you are becoming.
- **F — Faith:** Spiritual grounding, belief, and trust. Quiet strength, not performance.
- **T — Tomorrow (Coming Soon):** Future-focused storytelling centered on youth and the next generation. Informational only until launch.

---

## 3. Brand Essence

**What We Are:** Rooted · Elevated · Intentional · Empowering

**What We Are Not:** Trend-driven · Loud or gimmicky · Mass-produced in spirit · Performative

---

## 4. Voice & Tone

**Voice:** Grounded · Affirming · Calm but confident · Culturally fluent

**Tone Rules:**
- Never preachy
- Never trendy for trend's sake
- Emotionally intelligent
- Clear and intentional

## 5. Visual Identity

### Color System (Locked)

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | #B27F2C | Premium accents, purpose (reserved — not dominant) |
| Soft Yellow | #F7DE7B | Warmth, optimism |
| Royal Purple | #92278F | Royalty, depth (emphasis, not dominance) |
| Vibrant Magenta | #DB27DF | Energy, emphasis |
| Pink Accent | #EE2AC5 | Expression, softness |
| Deep Wine | #9E1F63 | Legacy, richness |
| Black | #000000 | Grounding base across web and apparel |
| White | #FFFFFF | Breathing space, clarity, contrast |

### Typography

- **Logo:** Script font for "Dorothy Dean" (identity, elegance) + Bold sans-serif for "DESIGNS" (strength)
- **Body/UI:** Clean, modern sans-serif for headers, CTAs, product names
- **Rules:** No novelty/decorative fonts. Script never used for long-form text.

## 7. Product & SKU Governance

**SKU Format (Locked):** `DDD-[ARTID]-[PRODUCT]-[FIT]-[COLOR]-[INK]`

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| UNI | Unisex |
| W | Women's |
| M | Men's (future) |

**Rules:** One Art ID per concept. No Art ID reuse. Multiple SKUs per color/product allowed.

## 8. Target Customer

- Faith-driven professionals, ages 25–40
- 62% female, 38% male
- 28% Black, 22% Hispanic
- $75K+ HHI: 52%
- Weekly apparel spend: ~$85
- Challenges: outdated Christian tees, no versatile faith-wear, fast-fashion ethics, cultural invisibility

---

## 9. Website Principles

**Above the Fold:** Clear value statement · LIFT framing · Primary CTA visible without scroll

**Primary CTAs:** "View LIFT" · "Shop Apparel"

**Routing Rules:** Informational pages before commerce when needed. No dead-end buttons.

---

## 10. Social Media System

- **Instagram Highlights:** Single highlight "LIFT" covering Legacy, Identity, Faith, Tomorrow
- **Content Rhythm:** Brand storytelling · Product context (not pushing) · Community-forward visuals

---

## 11. Business Model

- **Primary:** Print-on-demand via Shopify (direct-to-customer, no inventory risk)
- **Accessories:** Hats, jewelry, bags, phone cases
- **Digital:** Canva templates, calculators, guides
- **Custom:** Bulk and group orders (boutique differentiator)
- **Year 1 Goal:** $100K revenue (~1,500 customers, $67 AOV, 65% gross margin)
